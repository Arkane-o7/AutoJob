# Scout landing concept 01 — Editorial command center

Use case: ui-mockup

Asset type: high-fidelity desktop landing-page concept art for the Scout Chrome extension

Primary request: Design a world-class, launch-ready desktop homepage hero for Scout. Scout automatically fills job applications and manages the user's entire job search. The visual must sell the product immediately, not teach best practices and not lead with safety/review language.

Art direction: premium editorial command center; bold, intelligent, distinctive, slightly kinetic. Preserve Scout's warm ivory paper, black ink, electric acid-lime accents, subtle grid, elegant high-contrast display serif, compact utilitarian labels, hairline rules, crisp rectangular controls, and restrained coral status accents. Avoid generic purple SaaS gradients, glassmorphism, 3D blobs, stock people, or mascots.

Composition: landscape desktop website at roughly 16:10. Hero copy occupies about 42% of the left side. A layered product composition occupies the right: one browser application-form panel visibly completing ordinary fields, connected by a lime motion line into a faithful Scout pipeline/dashboard panel showing organized applications and statuses. Include status chips reading “12 fields filled” and “Saved to Scout”.

Text: “SCOUT”; “THE JOB SEARCH, HANDLED”; “Apply faster. Keep everything moving.”; “Scout fills job applications and keeps every role, deadline, and follow-up in one place.”; “Add to Chrome”; “See how it works”; “Built for the job hunt.”; and the beginning of a proof strip reading “FILL. TRACK. MOVE.”

Constraints: preserve the Scout identity rather than inventing a new logo; use plausible real product UI based on the supplied dashboard reference; no pricing, testimonials, privacy lecture, “review-first” headline, “AI copilot”, unrelated logos, or watermark.
