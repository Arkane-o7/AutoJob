## Final voiceover and dialogue

1. **Application purgatory**
   - Host: “Are you unemployed? Tired of typing the same details into job portal after job portal?”

2. **The phone interruption**
   - Host: “Still scrolling Instagram like a recruiter is about to discover you?”

3. **The career summit**
   - Host: “Do your employed friends turn brunch into an unsolicited career summit?”

4. **Broke / reveal**
   - Host: “And are you just… goddamn broke? If yes—oh boy, do I have something for you.”

5. **Scout product reveal**
   - Host: “Scout autofills repetitive job applications and keeps every role, deadline, and follow-up in one place.”

6. **Free little Timmy**
   - Host: “And you know what the best part is? It’s free.”
   - Timmy: “Free?”
   - Host: “Yes. Free, little Timmy.”

7. **CTA**
   - Host: “Click the link and add Scout to Chrome. Go on. It’s free. What excuse do you have?”
