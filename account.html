Before you connect

Scout can create, update, and remove events on calendars you own. Event details are stored in your Google Calendar. Scout uses this access only for your primary calendar and queries only events carrying Scout’s private action identifiers; it does not import or browse unrelated events. Disconnecting stops future synchronization but leaves existing Google events in place.
